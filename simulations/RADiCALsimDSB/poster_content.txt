================================================================================
 PART A — ONE-PARAGRAPH PROJECT SUMMARY (for the abstract / intro block)
================================================================================

RADiCAL is a radiation-hard, ultracompact "shashlik" electromagnetic calorimeter
module (LYSO crystal / tungsten sampling stack, ~22 X0 in 12.5 cm) read out by
wavelength-shifting (WLS) fibers in quartz capillaries, designed for precision
energy AND timing measurement in high-radiation, high-pileup environments (e.g. a
future high-luminosity forward calorimeter). This work builds a full Geant4
(v11.4.0, FTFP_BERT) simulation of the module and its CERN May-2023 SPS H2 test
beam (25–150 GeV electrons), and — critically — reconstructs the simulated signals
with the SAME systematic chain used on the real DRS4 test-beam data, so simulation
and experiment can be compared on identical axes. The headline observable is the
double-ended fiber timing resolution via the MCP-independent (DW−UP)/2 estimator.

 PART B — SUGGESTED POSTER STRUCTURE (blocks + short copy)

TITLE (suggested):
  "Simulating and Validating Precision Timing in the RADiCAL Shashlik Calorimeter"
  (alt: "Matching Geant4 to Test Beam: Energy and Timing in the RADiCAL Calorimeter")

BLOCK 1 — MOTIVATION
  - Future colliders (HL-LHC forward region, FCC) need calorimeters that survive
    extreme radiation AND resolve pileup in time (~30 ps class timing).
  - RADiCAL = radiation-hard LYSO/W shashlik with WLS-fiber readout; demonstrated
    ~37 ps timing at 150 GeV in the 2023 test beam.
  - Goal here: a Geant4 model reconstructed IDENTICALLY to the data, so the sim is a
    predictive design tool, not just a cross-check.

BLOCK 2 — THE DETECTOR
  - Stack: 29 LYSO plates (1.5 mm) + 28 W absorber (2.5 mm) + Tyvek wraps
    (0.2032 mm); total 124.88 mm ≈ 22 X0; 14×14 mm transverse.
  - 1 central capillary: EJ309 (energy sampling).
  - 4 corner capillaries (timing): DSB1 polysiloxane WLS fiber (fast ~3.5 ns decay,
    15 mm) at shower max, spliced to SOLID fused-quartz light-guide rods that pipe
    the light to photodetectors at BOTH ends (upstream + downstream) → double-ended
    readout enables the depth-timing trick.
  - CERN test-beam line modeled: trigger scintillators, MCP-PMT time reference,
    Pb-glass tail-catcher, wire chambers.
  - DAQ: 2× CAEN DT5742 (PSI DRS4 switched-capacitor, 5 GS/s, 1024 samples/ch).

BLOCK 3 — THE (DW−UP)/2 TIMING METHOD  [the physics centerpiece]
  - Each corner fiber is read at both ends. For light emitted at depth z_emit:
        ΔT = t_downstream − t_upstream = −2·z_emit / v_g
    so the beam/emission TIME cancels and σ_t = σ(ΔT)/2 = σ(z_emit)/v_g.
  - MCP-independent: any common-mode jitter (beam arrival, MCP, DRS4 timebase within
    one readout group) subtracts out. This is why the real detector reaches ~37 ps.
  - Sim reproduces the data's exact estimator: 5% constant-fraction discriminator on
    the emulated waveform, 4 corners AVERAGED per event before differencing.

BLOCK 4 — SIM ↔ DATA: ALIGNED SYSTEMATICS
  - Same iterative Gaussian-core fits (±2σ, 4 iterations), same σ = √(a²/E ⊕ b²) form.
  - Same timing estimator (4-corner (DW−UP)/2, 5% CFD).
  - Documented divergences: data energy = fiber-light sum (sum_lg) with Pb-glass as a
    VETO; sim energy = full LYSO dE/dx with Pb-glass ADDED BACK (tail-catcher). The
    sim is the idealized calorimetric limit; the data is light-collection limited.

BLOCK 5 — RESULTS: ENERGY
  - Sim full-module energy resolution: σ/E = 14.4%/√E ⊕ 2.49%  (clean √E law, 5–150 GeV).
  - Longitudinal shower profiles follow the Longo (gamma) form; shower max drifts
    deeper as t_max ∝ ln E.

BLOCK 6 — RESULTS: TIMING  [headline]
  - Sim (scint/WLS light, 5% CFD, (DW−UP)/2):  ~125/√E ⊕ ~14–18 ps.
  - Data (test beam, same estimator):          181/√E ⊕ 34.9 ps  (~37 ps @150 GeV).
  - Published (arXiv:2401.01747):              256/√E ⊕ 17.5 ps.
  - The sim's constant (high-E) floor from the light physics (~14–18 ps) sits right
    on the published value; the data's larger ~35 ps floor is uncalibrated DRS4 /
    electronics, not light.

BLOCK 7 — KEY PHYSICS INSIGHTS  (the "so what")
  (a) The 500 ps → 37 ps reconciliation: a naive single-channel/MCP-referenced
      analysis of the raw data gives ~500 ps (dominated by uncalibrated DRS4
      inter-cell timing). The MCP-free (DW−UP)/2 estimator, kept WITHIN one DRS4
      readout group, cancels that timebase error → ~37 ps. The gap was the
      *estimator*, not the light.
  (b) Cherenkov and the solid capillary: the quartz light-guide rods are SOLID, so
      they DO radiate Cherenkov and the photodetector sees it. In the real device
      scintillation/WLS light outnumbers Cherenkov ~1000:1, so the WLS light sets the
      leading edge and timing is excellent. In the sim the LYSO/WLS yield is scaled
      down 1000× for tractability while Cherenkov runs at full rate — INVERTING the
      ratio, so prompt Cherenkov artificially dominates and degrades all-light σ_t.
      Physics of the degradation: Cherenkov is emitted ALONG the whole rod, tracking
      the fluctuating shower, so its effective emission depth z_emit is broadly and
      randomly spread; since σ_t = σ(z_emit)/v_g, that spread inflates the constant
      term. WLS light, pinned to a fixed 15 mm window, does not. (Restoring the yield
      ratio → all-light converges to the WLS-dominated value: in-progress sweep.)
  (c) The shower-max "upturn": a fixed-depth energy slice worsens above ~50 GeV
      because t_max drifts past the fixed window (a hard-cut proxy artifact, much
      weaker for smooth light-based sampling — so absent in the real WLS readout).

BLOCK 8 — CONCLUSIONS / OUTLOOK
  - A Geant4 model reconstructed identically to the data reproduces the RADiCAL
    energy resolution and the light-limited timing floor.
  - Timing is set by emission POSITION spread, not light speed: the double-ended
    (DW−UP)/2 method is the key to the ~30 ps class result.
  - Next: LYSO-yield sweep to confirm all-light→WLS convergence; inject realistic
    DRS4 cell/noise to reproduce the data's ~35 ps electronics floor from first
    principles.

 CAVEATS (must survive into the poster's fine print — do not drop)
================================================================================
  1. Sim "all light" σ_t is inflated at the default LYSO-yield scale (1e-3): quartz
     Cherenkov is over-weighted ~1000× vs WLS. A yield sweep to demonstrate
     convergence to the WLS-dominated value is IN PROGRESS. Quote scint/WLS as the
     physically representative sim timing until the sweep confirms convergence.
  2. Sim fiber-light energy σ/E (~37%) is sparse-fiber-sampling limited (4 point
     fibers, 2.9 mm Gaussian beam spot) — NOT a fair absolute match to the data's
     tracked, fiducial-selected sum_lg (11–19%). Use the full-module ECombined
     (2.5%) as the sim energy number; the fiber-light plot is a method illustration.
  3. Data σ_t = 181/√E ⊕ 34.9 ps and σ/E = 11–19% are from the RADiCAL analysis
     repo (README/PLAN); re-derive from the raw data if quoting to <1 ps precision.

\block{Key Insight: Cherenkov \& the Solid Capillary}{
  % TODO: PART B, Block 7(b). This is the standout physics story.
